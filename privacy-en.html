Privacy Policy

Effective: August 5, 2026 / Last updated: September 5, 2026

Pano Hunt does not collect any personal information such as your name or email address. No account is required, and no internet connection is needed to play (a connection is required only to buy or restore an in-app purchase). Scores and settings are stored only on your device.

1. About the developer

Pano Hunt (the "App") is a 360° hidden-object game developed and operated by an independent developer (the "Developer").

2. Information we collect

The App does not collect any personal information, including your name, email address, phone number, or location. No account registration is required.

The App does not serve advertising and does not collect analytics or crash reports. No third-party SDKs providing those functions are embedded in the App.

In-app purchases are handled through Apple's App Store (StoreKit), which provides no advertising or behavioural analytics.

3. Information stored on your device

The following is stored only on your device and is never transmitted anywhere.

  • Your best score for each world
  • Settings (music, sound effects, haptics, display language)
  • Whether the tutorial has been shown
  • Whether an additional world has been purchased
  • How many trial plays remain
  • Whether a review prompt has already been shown

This data is included in the standard iOS backup (such as iCloud Backup) so that your records carry over when you change devices. The Developer cannot see its contents. You can exclude it yourself from the iOS settings if you prefer.

4. Data sharing and third parties

  • No connection is needed to play. Every world is fully playable in airplane mode.
  • An internet connection is required only to buy or restore an in-app purchase. These are handled through Apple's App Store.
  • We never provide your information to third parties.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.

5. In-app purchases and advertising

The App offers one-time in-app purchases that unlock additional worlds. A purchase is permanent — there is no subscription and no further charge. With the same Apple ID you can use "Restore purchases" after reinstalling or changing devices.

All payments are handled by Apple's App Store. The Developer never receives your credit card number or any other payment details.

The App serves no advertising.

6. Children's privacy

Because the App collects no personal information at all, no personal information reaches the Developer or any third party, including when the App is used by children.

7. Deleting your data

All data is stored only on your device, so deleting the App removes all of it.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be revised in response to changes in law or to added features. Significant changes will be announced on this page.
